# Data source, scope and processing

## Acquisition

1. Download the 2014 archive from the City of Melbourne open data portal
   (dataset `mq3i-cbxd`, CC BY 4.0):
   https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/parking-bay-arrivals-and-departures-2014/
2. Verify the archive SHA-256:
   `B92333B236D5DA0FBDC1AB0A195E57EB7A90EA4A3FDA30D06CF9938A3AD02A34`
   (13,503,655 rows, 236 MB compressed).
3. Extract or stream the single CSV `Parking_bay_arrivals_and_departures_2014.csv`.

Timestamps are local Melbourne time; 2014 contains both AEDT (UTC+11) and AEST (UTC+10), with
DST transitions that must be handled per local date, not by a fixed offset.

## Processing rules

- Candidate areas are filtered by observed bays and event counts; the study keeps ten sites,
  each an aggregation of 3-17 block faces (12-82 observed bays), with 825 site-day blocks and
  789 complete site-days in the adopted set.
- Two rows with epoch departure timestamps are dropped (Victoria Market 2014-03-14, Hyatt
  2014-01-15), giving 185,550 adopted events from 185,552 window rows.
- Capacity is measured as the maximum number of simultaneously parked distinct BayIDs per
  site-day (net intervals, departures first); it is observed supply, not registered capacity.
- Dwell is `departure - arrival` in minutes, excluding negative and epoch values.
- `InViolation` is retained as a source compliance flag (10.58% of adopted rows; median dwell
  133.3 min against 17.2 min for unflagged rows). The exclusion sensitivity changes the ideal
  energy service rate at 22 kW from 55.1% to 50.1%, so it is reported, not hidden.
- The EV, charging-willingness and energy fields are generated by the declared scenario
  (`apps/parking_lab/workbench/publicdata.py`, seed 20261031): EV share 0.10, charge need 0.36
  of EVs, energy from a truncated normal (14, 6) kWh on [2, 40]. They are assumptions.
