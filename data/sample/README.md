# Sample extract

300 rows from the processed window extract (`_window_events.csv`), the first 300 rows of
the file, published only as a runnable example.

- Source: City of Melbourne parking bay arrivals and departures 2014, CC BY 4.0.
- Columns: `event_id, device_id, bay_id, street, area, side, arr_local, dep_local, dur_s,
  viol_flag, arrival_utc, dwell_min, day_local`.
- Rows are real observations; no EV or energy field is present here. EV/charging fields are
  scenario-generated by the code, not by this file.
