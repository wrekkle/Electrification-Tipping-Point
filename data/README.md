# Data

## Source

City of Melbourne, *Parking bay arrivals and departures 2014* (dataset `mq3i-cbxd`),
licensed CC BY 4.0. Portal:
https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/parking-bay-arrivals-and-departures-2014/

The archive used for the study is
`melbourne_parking_bay_arrivals_departures_2014_mq3i-cbxd.zip` (236 MB, 13,503,655 rows,
5,228 parking bays, 335 block faces, 366 local dates). Raw archive SHA-256:
`B92333B236D5DA0FBDC1AB0A195E57EB7A90EA4A3FDA30D06CF9938A3AD02A34`.

The raw archive is not stored in this repository. The download and verification steps are in
`../docs/data.md` and `../docs/reproduce.md`.

## What is real and what is scenario

Real, observed: arrival time, departure time, dwell time, street, area, side, bay identifier,
InViolation flag.

Scenario layer (not observed): whether a vehicle is electric (`is_ev`), whether it needs a
charge (`needs_charge`), and the requested energy. These are generated by the declared
scenario in the code (`apps/parking_lab/workbench/publicdata.py`) with a recorded seed; they
are assumptions, not measured EV behaviour. No EV labels exist in the source data.
